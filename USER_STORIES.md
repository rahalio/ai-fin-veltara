# Veltara — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Head of digital wealth

- As a head of digital wealth, I want AI and advisor strategies filtered to each client’s risk profile before display, so that growth does not create mass unsuitable advice.
- As a head of digital wealth, I want to compare conversion and retention of governed AI advice versus unmanaged copytrading, so that I can justify the compliance investment.

### Licensed advisor

- As a licensed advisor, I want to review AI draft ideas, adjust thesis and risk bounds, and publish under my name, so that I remain accountable for advice I sponsor.
- As a licensed advisor, I want to see which of my published strategies were rejected by suitability gates, so that I can fix packaging rather than argue with compliance after the fact.

### Retail client (via host app)

- As a client, I want to understand why a strategy was recommended for me and what can go wrong, so that I am not blindly copying strangers.
- As a client, I want to set hard loss limits that block execution even if I click “follow,” so that enthusiasm cannot override my risk budget.

### Compliance / advice supervisor

- As a compliance officer, I want to freeze a toxic strategy across all clients immediately, so that conduct exposure is capped.
- As a compliance officer, I want an exportable advice audit for any client and period, so that we can answer a regulator without reconstructing chat logs.
- As an advice supervisor, I want dual-control override when an advisor insists on presenting a near-miss strategy, so that exceptions are rare and evidenced.

### Risk officer

- As a risk officer, I want strategy-level risk statistics recalculated on a defined schedule, so that stale Sharpe claims cannot persist as live advice.
- As a risk officer, I want alerts when aggregate client following of one strategy breaches concentration limits, so that herd risk is visible before a drawdown.

### Platform administrator

- As a platform administrator, I want to onboard advisors with license attestations and product permissions, so that only authorized persons publish advice.
- As a platform administrator, I want model versions retired with a recorded successor, so that we never silently serve an unapproved model.
